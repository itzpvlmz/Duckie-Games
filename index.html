Who We Are

Crafting experiences that stick with players

Built on Passion

Duckie Games is an independent game development studio founded by Logan Ancell in 2026. Our mission is to turn creative visions into unforgettable playable experiences.

What began as a dream to craft original worlds has evolved into a vibrant hub for passionate creators. We believe in building games with heart, personality, and genuine staying power.

Current Focus: Developing original indie games while building a creative community of artists, programmers, designers, and storytellers who share our vision.

Our Philosophy

We create experiences that matter. Every project is built on three pillars:

🌍 Memorable Worlds

Building worlds filled with mystery, adventure, and discovery.

✨ Meaningful Adventures

Creating experiences players remember long after they finish.

👥 Creative Community

Bringing creators and players together around projects with personality.
