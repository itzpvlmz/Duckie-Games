About Duckie Games

The story behind our studio and the games we're building

Founded in 2026

Duckie Games was born from a simple idea: turn creative visions into memorable playable experiences. Founded by Logan Ancell, the studio started as a passion project to build original games that stick with players.

What began as a dream has grown into a collaborative space where passionate creators come together to build worlds worth exploring. We're not just making games—we're crafting experiences that players will remember long after the final screen.

🎮

Our Mission

We believe in creating original indie games with heart, personality, and genuine staying power. Each project is built from the ground up with a focus on meaningful storytelling, engaging gameplay, and worlds that feel alive.

Our mission is to bring together passionate creators—artists, programmers, designers, and storytellers—to build something bigger than any one person could alone. We're growing a creative community united by a shared vision.

🌍

Our Journey

Key milestones as we build Duckie Games

June 2026 - Studio Foundation

Duckie Games officially launches as an independent studio. Our website goes live, and we begin recruiting passionate collaborators.

June 2026 - Development Begins

Croak & Dagger enters active development. The core team starts building the foundation for our flagship dark fantasy adventure.

Q3 2026 - Pre-Production Complete

Finalize core game direction, visual style, and story. Systems planning and prototype work begins.

Q4 2026 - First Playable Demo

A polished vertical slice showcasing core gameplay, combat feel, and visual direction.

2027 - Community Feedback

Open playtest opportunities, community updates, and iteration based on player feedback.
